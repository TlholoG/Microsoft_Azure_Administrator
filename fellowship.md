# 9. Before the restricted application, I suggest building your own "AI policy map"

Not an application answer.

### Compute and chips

### Export controls

### Verification

How could governments or international organisations verify what AI capabilities or systems organisations possess or are developing?

### Frontier AI governance

How should governments deal with increasingly capable AI systems whose capabilities may outpace existing regulatory structures?

### Government involvement in AI

What should governments actually do themselves versus leave to private companies?

### Forecasting and uncertainty

How should policymakers make decisions when the technology is developing rapidly and evidence is incomplete?

That last one might be particularly worth exploring given your mathematics/data background.
